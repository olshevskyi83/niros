# Sprint 001 — Paper Analysis

## Paper

**Kajonius, P. J., & Johnson, J. A. (2019)**

**Title:** Assessing the Structure of the Five Factor Model of Personality (IPIP-NEO-120) in the Public Domain

**Status:** Foundation

---

# Objective

Evaluate whether the IPIP-NEO-120 provides a valid and robust hierarchical representation of the Five Factor Model.

---

# Evidence

## 1. Big Five structure is confirmed.

Analysis of more than 320,000 participants supports the robustness of the Five Factor Model.  [oai_citation:1‡ejop-15-260.pdf](sediment://file_0000000013c0720eb4c3228a38a57bdd)

**Confidence:** High

---

## 2. Facets provide additional precision.

Thirty lower-level facets improve the description of personality beyond the five global domains.  [oai_citation:2‡ejop-15-260.pdf](sediment://file_0000000013c0720eb4c3228a38a57bdd)

**Confidence:** High

---

## 3. Not all facets contribute equally.

Some facets show weaker relationships with their parent domains, whereas others are extremely strong indicators.  [oai_citation:3‡ejop-15-260.pdf](sediment://file_0000000013c0720eb4c3228a38a57bdd)

**Confidence:** High

---

## 4. Core facets exist.

Traits such as Self-discipline, Friendliness and Anxiety show exceptionally strong relationships with their corresponding Big Five domains.  [oai_citation:4‡ejop-15-260.pdf](sediment://file_0000000013c0720eb4c3228a38a57bdd)

**Confidence:** High

---

# NIROS Interpretation

- Big Five should remain the top-level personality model.
- Lower-level facets provide the precision required for personalised assessment.
- NIROS should prioritise the most informative facets rather than treating all facets equally.

---

# Limitations

- Focuses on personality structure only.
- Does not evaluate therapeutic outcomes.
- Does not assess conversational AI methods.

---

# Working Hypotheses

### H001

NIROS should model personality hierarchically:

- Big Five Domains
- Personality Facets

**Status:** Tentative

---

### H002

Different facets should contribute with different importance during personality estimation.

**Status:** Tentative

---

### H003

Future NIROS models should learn which facets best predict therapeutic outcomes.

**Status:** Tentative

---

# Open Questions
