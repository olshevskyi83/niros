# Objective

Determine whether the International Personality Item Pool (IPIP) can serve as the psychometric foundation for NIROS.

---

# Evidence

## 1. IPIP was created to solve a scientific problem.

The project was created because personality research depended heavily on proprietary questionnaires that could not easily be improved or compared.

IPIP provides an open framework where personality measures can continuously evolve through scientific collaboration.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 2. IPIP is based on the Big Five framework.

The entire project uses the Big Five as the primary organisational structure while allowing measurement of many lower-level personality facets.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 3. IPIP has grown into a large psychometric ecosystem.

The project currently contains:

- more than 3,000 personality items
- more than 250 scales
- translations into many languages
- hundreds of scientific publications

It is no longer a single questionnaire but a complete psychometric platform.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 4. Reliability is well documented.

Reliability is reported using Cronbach's alpha for individual scales.

Construct validity is primarily evaluated by comparing IPIP scales with established personality inventories.

**Confidence:** High

---

## 5. Continuous development.

IPIP is designed to evolve.

New items, scales and validation studies are continuously added as new evidence becomes available.

**Confidence:** High

---

# NIROS Interpretation

IPIP should not be viewed as a questionnaire.

It should be viewed as a psychometric library.

NIROS does not need thousands of questions.

Instead, NIROS can select only those items that provide the greatest information for adaptive interviewing.

The long-term objective should not be reproducing IPIP.

The objective should be building an intelligent adaptive assessment system inspired by its scientific principles.

---

# Limitations

- IPIP measures personality rather than psychopathology.
- It does not include psychedelic-specific constructs.
- It was not designed for conversational AI.
- Many available scales overlap in content.

---

# Working Hypotheses

### H001

IPIP will become the primary source of psychometric items during NIROS development.

**Status:** Tentative

---

### H002

NIROS should estimate personality adaptively rather than administer complete inventories.

**Status:** Tentative

---

### H003

Only a small subset of IPIP items will ultimately be required for NIROS.

**Status:** Tentative
