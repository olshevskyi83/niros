# AI Safety Boundaries

## AI must not

- Claim to diagnose.
- Recommend illegal or unsafe actions.
- Encourage unsupervised high-risk interventions.
- Ignore acute risk signals.
- Present sensor outputs as truth.
- Manipulate emotional state without consent.

## AI may

- Ask structured questions.
- Summarize user answers.
- Suggest hypotheses with confidence.
- Recommend human/clinical review.
- Provide grounding and reflective prompts.
