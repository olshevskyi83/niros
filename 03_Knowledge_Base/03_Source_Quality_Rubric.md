# Source Quality Rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Strong source: systematic review, high-quality RCT, official clinical guidance |
| 4 | Good source: controlled study or strong observational evidence |
| 3 | Useful but limited: small study, mechanistic paper, expert clinical framework |
| 2 | Weak: opinion, non-systematic essay, unclear methods |
| 1 | Not reliable for claims; maybe useful only as inspiration |

## Required judgment

For each source, record:

- What it actually supports.
- What it does not support.
- Whether it applies to NIROS users.
- Whether the finding is clinical, mechanistic, or product-adjacent.
