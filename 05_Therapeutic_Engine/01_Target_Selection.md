# Target Selection

Therapeutic targets are not symptoms. They are change directions.

## Examples

| Symptom | Possible target |
|---|---|
| Low mood | reconnect with values and agency |
| Anxiety | build regulation and uncertainty tolerance |
| Burnout | reduce overload and restore recovery |
| Grief | support meaning-making and emotional processing |
| Trauma pattern | increase safety, stabilization, and integration |

## Rule

Targets must be tied to evidence from Human Understanding Engine.
