# Confidence Scoring

Confidence scoring should be transparent and conservative.

## Inputs

- Symptom fit
- Timeline fit
- Functional impact
- Contradictory evidence
- Risk signals
- Scale results
- Sensor context
- Missing data

## Suggested scoring categories

| Score | Label |
|---:|---|
| 0.00–0.39 | weak |
| 0.40–0.59 | possible |
| 0.60–0.79 | likely |
| 0.80–1.00 | strong working hypothesis |

## Rule

High confidence requires both supporting evidence and low contradiction.
