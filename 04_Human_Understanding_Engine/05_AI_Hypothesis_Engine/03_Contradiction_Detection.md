# Contradiction Detection

Contradictions are not proof of deception. They are signals to ask better questions.

## Types

- Self-report contradiction
- Timeline contradiction
- Emotion-word mismatch
- Declared problem vs evidence mismatch
- Sensor-state mismatch

## Example

User says: “I am calm.”

Sensor context: HR rises, HRV drops, voice pauses increase.

NIROS response should not be: “You are lying.”

Better:

> “This topic may be affecting you more than it first seemed. Would it be okay to slow down and explore what came up?”
