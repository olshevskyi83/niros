# Termination Rules

The interview must know when to stop.

## Stop conditions

- Required profile fields are sufficiently complete.
- Risk escalation is triggered.
- User wants to stop.
- Maximum question count reached.
- The system is looping or asking repetitive questions.
- Confidence is sufficient for next-step recommendation.

## Output on termination

- reason_for_stop
- completed_domains
- incomplete_domains
- confidence_summary
- risk_status
- recommended_next_step
