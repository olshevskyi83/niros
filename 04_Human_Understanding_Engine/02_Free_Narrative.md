# Free Narrative

The free narrative is the first unstructured window into the person's experience.

## Prompt examples

- “Tell me what brought you here.”
- “What feels most difficult right now?”
- “When did this start to become a problem?”
- “What would you most want to change?”

## What the AI extracts

- Main themes
- Emotional tone
- Timeline
- Repeated words or motifs
- People and relationships mentioned
- Functional impairment
- Risk hints
- Contradictions or uncertainty

## Output structure

```yaml
narrative_summary:
key_themes:
timeline:
affected_domains:
initial_hypotheses:
risk_hints:
followup_priorities:
```

## Important

This is not diagnosis. It is hypothesis generation.
