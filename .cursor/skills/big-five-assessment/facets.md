# Big Five Facets Reference

Standard **NEO-PI-R facet model** (Costa & McCrae). Use for item tagging, adaptive probing, and explainable output.

---

## Openness (O)

| Code | Facet | Definition | Example item stem (NIROS tone) |
|------|-------|------------|--------------------------------|
| **O1** | Fantasy | Vivid imagination, inner life | "I enjoy daydreaming and imagining different possibilities." |
| **O2** | Aesthetics | Appreciation of art, beauty, nature | "I notice and appreciate beauty in everyday things." |
| **O3** | Feelings | Receptivity to inner emotions | "I am in touch with my emotions and inner experiences." |
| **O4** | Actions | Willingness to try new activities | "I like trying activities that are new to me." |
| **O5** | Ideas | Intellectual curiosity | "I enjoy exploring ideas even when they are abstract or complex." |
| **O6** | Values | Readiness to re-examine social, political, religious values | "I am open to reconsidering my beliefs when I encounter new perspectives." |

---

## Conscientiousness (C)

| Code | Facet | Definition | Example item stem |
|------|-------|------------|-------------------|
| **C1** | Competence | Sense of capability, effectiveness | "I feel capable of handling my responsibilities." |
| **C2** | Order | Organization, neatness, planning | "I like to keep my space and tasks organized." |
| **C3** | Dutifulness | Adherence to ethical obligation | "I follow through on commitments I make to others." |
| **C4** | Achievement Striving | Aspiration, work ethic | "I push myself to do well on tasks that matter to me." |
| **C5** | Self-Discipline | Ability to begin and persist | "I can stay focused even when a task is boring or difficult." |
| **C6** | Deliberation | Think before acting | "I think carefully before making important decisions." |

---

## Extraversion (E)

| Code | Facet | Definition | Example item stem |
|------|-------|------------|-------------------|
| **E1** | Warmth | Affiliation, closeness to others | "I warm up quickly to people I meet." |
| **E2** | Gregariousness | Preference for company | "I enjoy being around groups of people." |
| **E3** | Assertiveness | Dominance, social leadership | "I speak up comfortably in group discussions." |
| **E4** | Activity | Pace of life, energy | "I prefer to stay busy rather than idle." |
| **E5** | Excitement-Seeking | Need for stimulation, thrill | "I enjoy situations that feel exciting or stimulating." |
| **E6** | Positive Emotions | Tendency toward joy, optimism | "I often feel cheerful and upbeat." |

---

## Agreeableness (A)

| Code | Facet | Definition | Example item stem |
|------|-------|------------|-------------------|
| **A1** | Trust | Belief in others' good intentions | "I generally trust people until given reason not to." |
| **A2** | Straightforwardness | Frankness, sincerity | "I prefer to be direct rather than hide what I think." |
| **A3** | Altruism | Active concern for others | "I go out of my way to help when someone is struggling." |
| **A4** | Compliance | Deference, conflict avoidance | "I avoid arguments even when I disagree." |
| **A5** | Modesty | Humility, not boasting | "I do not like to draw attention to my achievements." *(reverse-key candidate)* |
| **A6** | Tender-Mindedness | Empathy, concern for the vulnerable | "I am moved when others are treated unfairly." |

| **N2** | Angry Hostility | Irritability, frustration | "I get irritated more easily than most people." |

## Reverse-keyed example pairs

Use paired items to detect inconsistency and reduce acquiescence bias. One positive, one reverse per facet where possible.

| Facet | Positive-keyed | Reverse-keyed |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| C2 Order | "I like to keep my tasks organized." | "I rarely bother organizing my things." |
| E2 Gregariousness | "I enjoy being around groups of people." | "I prefer to spend most of my time alone." |
| N1 Anxiety | "I often worry about things that might go wrong." | "I rarely feel tense or worried." |
| A4 Compliance | "I avoid arguments even when I disagree." | "I stand my ground even if it starts a conflict." |

## Short-form coverage (NIROS MVP)

A minimal **10-item domain screen** can sample facets as:

| Domain | Suggested facets for screen item |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| O | O4 (Actions), O5 (Ideas) |
| C | C2 (Order), C5 (Self-Discipline) |
| E | E2 (Gregariousness), E6 (Positive Emotions) |
| A | A1 (Trust), A3 (Altruism) |
| N | N1 (Anxiety), N6 (Vulnerability) |

Adaptive probing then targets the facet with lowest confidence within the domain.
