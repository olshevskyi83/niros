# Clinical Research Overview

## Purpose

Design research that can test NeuroIcaro hypotheses responsibly.

## Study ladder

1. Technical feasibility.
2. Non-clinical acceptability study.
3. Healthy volunteer audio personalization study without psychedelics.
4. Observational psychedelic setting study where legal.
5. Pilot clinical study with ethics approval.
6. Randomized controlled trial.

## Primary outcomes for fibromyalgia

- pain interference;
- fatigue;
- sleep quality;
- quality of life;
- pain catastrophizing;
- adverse events.
