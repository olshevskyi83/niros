# DR-007 — Voice-First Intake

## Decision

NIROS should support voice-first questionnaire intake as a primary interaction mode.

## Rationale

Voice is more natural for deep personal disclosure and preserves emotional nuance that typed forms often lose.

## Constraints

Voice-derived markers are contextual only. They must not be treated as diagnosis, lie detection, or standalone clinical evidence.

## Consequences

The system must store raw transcripts, structured extraction, confidence, and safety flags separately.
