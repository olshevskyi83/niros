# DR-003: No Frequency Cure Claims

## Context

Audio is framed through attention, emotion, rhythm, and safety, not magic frequencies.

## Decision

Accepted as a guiding project decision.

## Consequences

- Shapes system architecture.
- Shapes prompts and documentation.
- Reduces risk of unsafe claims.
