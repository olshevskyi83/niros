# DR-002: Voice Biomarkers Are Not Lie Detection

## Context

Physiology and speech are interpreted as activation signals, not truth signals.

## Decision

Accepted as a guiding project decision.

## Consequences

- Shapes system architecture.
- Shapes prompts and documentation.
- Reduces risk of unsafe claims.
