# Addiction Protocol

## Primary targets

- craving loop,
- avoidance cycle,
- shame,
- emotional regulation deficit,
- meaning vacuum,
- relapse triggers.

## Intake focus

- substance/behavior type,
- frequency,
- withdrawal risk,
- medical risk,
- social support,
- relapse history,
- motivation stage.

## Safety rule

Acute withdrawal, severe dependence, or medical instability requires human/clinical review.
