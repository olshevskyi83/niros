# Depression Protocol

## Primary targets

- emotional numbness,
- hopelessness,
- anhedonia,
- grief,
- self-criticism,
- loss of meaning,
- exhaustion.

## Intake focus

- duration,
- severity,
- suicidality screen,
- sleep,
- energy,
- social isolation,
- previous treatment,
- medications,
- bipolar/mania screening.

## AI confirmation question

"You came with depression. From your answers, does the main pattern feel more like sadness, numbness, exhaustion, grief, self-hate, or loss of meaning?"

## Safety flags

- suicidality,
- psychosis,
- mania/hypomania,
- severe functional collapse,
- medication contraindications.
