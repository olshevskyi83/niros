# Existential Distress Protocol

## Primary targets

- loss of meaning,
- death anxiety,
- identity collapse,
- spiritual conflict,
- life transition,
- alienation.

## Intake focus

- worldview,
- beliefs/non-beliefs,
- symbols that resonate or repel,
- values,
- mortality themes,
- social connection.

## Personalization rule

This protocol must adapt to atheistic, religious, spiritual, indigenous, secular, and skeptical worldviews without forcing metaphysics.
