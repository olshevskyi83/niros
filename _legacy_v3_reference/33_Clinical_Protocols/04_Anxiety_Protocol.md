# Anxiety Protocol

## Primary targets

- chronic worry,
- panic/body threat sensitivity,
- control fear,
- social threat,
- uncertainty intolerance,
- somatic vigilance.

## Intake focus

- panic attacks,
- triggers,
- avoidance,
- body symptoms,
- sleep,
- caffeine/stimulants,
- trauma history.

## AI confirmation question

"You said anxiety. Does it feel more like mental worry, body panic, social fear, or fear of losing control?"
