# Voice Intake Signal Map

## Purpose

Voice input is used for both content capture and contextual analysis.

## Signal categories

### Semantic

- stated problem,
- life story fragments,
- emotional themes,
- preferred language,
- metaphors,
- worldview references,
- resistance or fear.

### Acoustic / prosodic

- pace,
- pause length,
- volume variation,
- monotonicity,
- trembling voice,
- breath interruptions,
- fatigue markers.

### Conversational behavior

- avoidance,
- over-explaining,
- uncertainty,
- rapid topic switching,
- emotional collapse,
- guardedness.

## Safety limitation

Voice signals must never be used as lie detection or diagnosis.

They are weak signals that can support:

- personalization,
- pacing,
- safety attention,
- session intensity adjustment.
