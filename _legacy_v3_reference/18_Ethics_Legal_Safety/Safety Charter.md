# Safety Charter

## Core safety principles

- Do not promise healing.
- Do not bypass medical care.
- Do not pressure users into psychedelic use.
- Do not use fake authority.
- Do not use voice cloning without consent.
- Do not use physiological data as a lie detector.
- Do not manipulate vulnerable people.

## Red lines

The system must refuse to generate content that intensifies delusions, paranoia, dependency, guru dynamics, or medical misinformation.
