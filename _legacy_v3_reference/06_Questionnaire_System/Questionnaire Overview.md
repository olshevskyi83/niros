# Questionnaire Overview

## Purpose

The questionnaire is the structured entry point for building the profile.

## Questionnaire layers

1. Identity and life context.
2. Clinical screening.
3. Pain and body relationship.
4. Trauma sensitivity.
5. Belief system and worldview.
6. Language and symbol preference.
7. Voice preference.
8. Music preference.
9. Trust and safety.
10. Integration goals.

The system should use adaptive questioning: answers open or close deeper branches.
