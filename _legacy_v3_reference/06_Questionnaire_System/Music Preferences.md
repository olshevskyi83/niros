# Music Preferences

## Goal

Collect information about **Music Preferences** for personalization and safety.

## Example questions

1. Describe this area in your own words.
2. What feels safe, neutral, or threatening here?
3. Which words or images should the system avoid?
4. Which words or images feel grounding?
5. How strongly does this affect your body from 0 to 10?

## Output variables

- relevance_score
- emotional_load
- safety_constraints
- preferred_language
- avoid_language
- followup_needed

## Voice mode

The participant may answer by voice. The system stores transcript, timestamps, pauses, and optional physiological context.
