# Research OS Overview

NIROS is not only an app. It is also a research operating system.

## Core research logs

```text
Decision Log
Hypothesis Log
Experiment Log
Paper Review Log
Adverse Event Log
Architecture Change Log
Cursor Build Log
```

## Why this matters

If NIROS becomes a long-term project, the historical record will become one of its most valuable assets.

## Required logs

- Every major architecture decision gets a decision record.
- Every research hypothesis gets a hypothesis note.
- Every experiment gets a structured experiment note.
- Every unsafe or failed output gets logged for red-team improvement.

## Cursor implementation notes

Eventually create UI pages for:

- adding a hypothesis;
- logging an experiment;
- linking a result to a paper/module;
- exporting research reports.
