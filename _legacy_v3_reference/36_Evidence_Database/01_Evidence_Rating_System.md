# Evidence Rating System

## Rating dimensions

- study type,
- sample size,
- replication status,
- clinical relevance,
- risk of bias,
- population match,
- effect size,
- uncertainty.

## Rating levels

- A — strong replicated clinical evidence,
- B — moderate clinical evidence,
- C — early or indirect evidence,
- D — theoretical or speculative,
- X — contradicted or unsafe.
