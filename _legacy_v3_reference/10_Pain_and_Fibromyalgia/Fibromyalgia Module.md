# Fibromyalgia Module

## Why fibromyalgia matters

Fibromyalgia is a serious condition involving chronic widespread pain, fatigue, sleep disturbance, cognitive symptoms, and altered pain processing.

## NeuroIcaro relevance

The project hypothesis is especially relevant to fibromyalgia because the condition involves central pain processing, threat perception, body interpretation, stress, sleep, and identity.

## Research question

Can a personalized acoustic-semantic protocol, delivered during a controlled psychedelic-assisted therapy framework, reduce pain interference and improve quality of life compared with standard supportive therapy?

## Target dimensions

- pain catastrophizing;
- body fear;
- learned helplessness;
- nervous system threat state;
- sleep safety;
- fatigue identity;
- movement fear;
- self-compassion and agency.
