# Audio Engine Overview

## Purpose

The Audio Engine converts the script into an acoustic delivery format.

## MVP

- recommended voice style;
- spoken script;
- optional simple melodic contour;
- manual audio export.

## Later versions

- TTS voice rendering;
- sung voice synthesis;
- adaptive tempo;
- dynamic intensity;
- biofeedback-linked audio modulation.

## Important rule

Do not claim that a specific frequency cures disease. Focus on attention, emotion, safety, rhythm, and embodiment.
