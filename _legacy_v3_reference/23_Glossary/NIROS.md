# NIROS

**NIROS** means **NeuroIcaro Research Operating System**.

NIROS is the internal name for the complete research, data, AI, safety, and development architecture behind NeuroIcaro.

## Relationship between NeuroIcaro and NIROS

- **NeuroIcaro** — public-facing project name / product identity.
- **NIROS** — internal operating system, architecture, data model, research engine, and development specification.

In practice, the project can be called **NIROS** from now on. NeuroIcaro remains a historical and branding layer.

## Core idea

NIROS is not a chatbot and not a digital shaman. It is a structured research operating system for analyzing, designing, documenting, and evaluating personalized psychedelic session support.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Human facilitator remains central.
2. AI supports research and personalization; it does not replace clinical judgment.
3. The system does not provide substance preparation, dosage, or unsupervised medical instructions.
4. Every generated output must be traceable to profile data, safety constraints, evidence notes, and module logic.
5. Data collection and outcome tracking are part of the core system, not an afterthought.
