# NeuroIcaro Bible

## One sentence definition

**NeuroIcaro** is a research platform for generating personalized acoustic-semantic therapeutic protocols based on a deep psychological, linguistic, physiological, and clinical profile of a person.

## Core hypothesis

Psilocybin may temporarily increase neuroplasticity. During this window, a personalized acoustic-semantic protocol may influence emotional, cognitive, and somatic patterns more effectively than generic therapeutic support.

## What is new here

The project does not treat psilocybin as the only active agent. It treats psilocybin as a possible **plasticity window opener**, while the personalized information structure becomes the main intervention candidate.

## Non-negotiable principles

- No magical claims.
- No promises of cure.
- No unsupervised clinical deployment.
- No fake objectivity from weak biomarkers.
- No “frequency heals disease” framing.
- Every claim must be labeled by evidence level.
