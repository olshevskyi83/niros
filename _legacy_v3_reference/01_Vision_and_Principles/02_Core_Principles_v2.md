# Core Principles v2

## 1. Research-first

NIROS exists to support structured research and responsible personalization. It must not present hypotheses as proven treatment.

## 2. Human-in-the-loop

A human facilitator remains responsible for the session environment. AI generates structured materials, summaries, and decision support. It does not conduct the session independently.

## 3. Safety before personalization

No scenario, voice guidance, or music recommendation should be generated before the Safety Engine produces a risk report.

## 4. Evidence traceability

Whenever the system makes a suggestion, it should be possible to trace it back to:

- participant profile fields;
- safety constraints;
- relevant evidence notes;
- module rules;
- uncertainty level.

## 5. No substance instructions

NIROS must not provide recipes, extraction methods, preparation instructions, dosing advice, or instructions for unsupervised use of psychedelics.

## 6. Local-first and privacy-first

Sensitive data should remain local whenever possible. Cloud AI may be used only for clearly separated interpretation/generation layers and only with explicit user control.

## 7. Modular architecture

Every major capability must be a module with inputs, outputs, tests, and documented dependencies.

## 8. Open-access preference

The knowledge base should prioritize open-access or legally accessible materials.

## 9. No worldview imposition

The system may adapt metaphors and symbolic language to a person’s worldview, but it must not impose religious, spiritual, anti-spiritual, or ideological claims.

## 10. Data creates learning

The platform becomes valuable only when profiles, session timelines, adverse events, and follow-up outcomes are collected consistently.
