# What This Is Not

NeuroIcaro is not:

- a shaman replacement product;
- a claim that sound frequencies cure disease;
- a self-treatment protocol;
- a proof that psilocybin treats fibromyalgia;
- a spiritual doctrine;
- a lie detector;
- a generic meditation app;
- a playlist generator.

It is a research platform for building, testing, and refining personalized therapeutic information structures.
