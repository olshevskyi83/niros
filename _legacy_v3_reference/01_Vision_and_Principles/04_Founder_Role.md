# Founder Role

The founder is not required to be a scientist or professional developer to lead NIROS.

The founder's role is:

- define the product vision;
- make practical decisions;
- protect ethical boundaries;
- test the system carefully;
- collect real-world needs;
- keep the vault organized;
- bring in experts later when needed.

## What the founder should not do alone

The founder should not independently claim clinical efficacy, run clinical treatment protocols, override safety flags, or present the system as medical care.

## Practical operating model

```text
Founder = product owner and system operator
Obsidian = source of truth
Cursor = implementation assistant
AI = reasoning and generation assistant
Future experts = validation and clinical/scientific review
```

## Key discipline

The founder does not need formal education to start. But the system must be built so that unsupported ideas are clearly separated from evidence, and every important decision is documented.
