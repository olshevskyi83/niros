# Patent Strategy Overview

## What may be patentable

Not the idea itself, but specific technical methods:

- a method for building a psychological-acoustic profile;
- a pipeline for generating personalized acoustic-semantic protocols;
- biofeedback-driven adaptation of therapeutic audio;
- structured safety constraints for psychedelic audio generation;
- a method for matching voice parameters to profile dimensions.

## Rule

Do not publicly disclose detailed implementation before speaking with a patent attorney if patent protection becomes a serious goal.
