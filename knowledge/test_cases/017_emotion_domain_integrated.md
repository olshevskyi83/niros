# Scenario
I push my feelings down so I can keep going.
My emotions feel too big to handle.
I often do not know what I am feeling.
I avoid situations that might upset me.
My mind gets stuck on the same worries.
Small things can spike my anxiety quickly.
I get overwhelmed by my feelings quickly.
I distract myself so I do not have to feel.
I keep going over the same problems in my head.
My body reacts with anxiety before I even know why.
I try not to feel anything when things get hard.
Emotions flood me before I can slow down.
