# Scenario
It is hard for me to fully trust people.
Even when someone is kind, I keep wondering what they really want from me.
I often expect people to disappoint me eventually.
I do not like depending on others because it feels unsafe.
Sometimes I keep emotional distance so I cannot be hurt.
