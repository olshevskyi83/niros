# Scenario
When someone praises me, I suddenly feel good about myself.
After criticism, I quickly feel worthless.
I track my value through how others react to me.
I am not sure whether I am good enough as a person.
A small failure can make my self-worth collapse.
When I achieve something, I feel valuable for a while.
I need approval to feel okay about myself.
Comparing myself to others decides how valuable I feel.
I often feel embarrassed even when no one is watching.
I beat myself up over small mistakes.
No matter what I achieve, it never feels good enough.
I probably cannot do this.
I do not really know who I am.
