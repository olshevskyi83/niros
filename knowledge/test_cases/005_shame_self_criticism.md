# Scenario
When I make a mistake, I feel like a failure.
I replay what I did wrong over and over in my head.
I often feel embarrassed even when no one is watching.
I am harsh with myself in ways I would never be with a friend.
Sometimes I want to disappear when I think I have let someone down.
# Expected Patterns
- shame_sensitivity
- harsh_self_criticism
# Expected Follow-up Domains
- self
- self-worth
- relationships
