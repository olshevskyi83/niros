# Scenario
I find it hard to say no when people ask me for something.
Even when I am exhausted, I still agree to help.
I often feel guilty when I put my own needs first.
Sometimes I let people cross my limits because I do not want to seem selfish.
Afterwards I feel resentful, but I usually do not say anything.
