# Scenario
When someone praises me, I suddenly feel good about myself.
After criticism, I quickly feel worthless.
I track my value through how others react to me.
I am not sure whether I am good enough as a person.
A small failure can make my self-worth collapse.
When I achieve something, I feel valuable for a while.
When I make a mistake, I feel like a failure.

- shame_sensitivity
