# Scenario
I hesitate to apply for opportunities because I assume I will fail.
I give up quickly when I hit a small setback.
When something goes well, I usually think it was luck or help from someone else.
I often ask other people to decide for me because I do not trust my own judgment.
I feel powerless to change my situation through my own actions.
I am harsh with myself for assuming I will fail before I even try.

- low_self_efficacy
- harsh_self_criticism
