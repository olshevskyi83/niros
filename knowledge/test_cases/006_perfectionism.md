# Scenario
I delay starting tasks because I am afraid the result will not be perfect.
Even small mistakes make me feel intensely ashamed.
When I finish something, I quickly dismiss it and move on to what still needs improvement.
No matter what I achieve, it never feels good enough.
I tell myself I should have done better.
If I make one error, it feels like it reveals something defective about me.
