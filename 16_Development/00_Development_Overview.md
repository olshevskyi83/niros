# Development Philosophy

## Primary Goal

NIROS is an engineering project whose purpose is to build a practical AI system that can help people.

The goal is not to conduct academic psychological research.

The goal is not to publish new psychological theories.

The goal is to build a working, useful and transparent product.

---

## Scientific Foundation

Scientific evidence is the foundation of NIROS.

However, engineering decisions should prioritize practical usefulness over theoretical completeness.

When scientific evidence is limited, prefer transparent and conservative engineering decisions rather than unnecessary complexity.

---

## MVP Philosophy

The first version of NIROS is expected to work without thousands of participants.

The platform should provide useful assessments from the beginning and improve incrementally through future validation and user feedback.

Avoid designing systems that require large datasets before becoming useful.

---

## Development Principles

- Build the smallest working solution first.
- Keep modules simple and independent.
- Validate through implementation.
- Avoid unnecessary architecture.
- Ship early and improve continuously.
- Prefer practical value over theoretical perfection.
- Every feature should make the MVP more useful.
