The classic psychedelic psilocybin has attracted special interest across clinical and non-clinical Received 29 July 2024

collected using validated measures and assessed alongside other measurements of psychological experience; emotional
health. Acute feelings of shame or guilt during psilocybin experiences were commonly reported breakthrough
(68.2% of users) and difficult to predict. Ratings of participant ability to constructively work through

a small but significant decrease in trait shame that was maintained 2-3 months after use (Cohen's
dz = 0.37). Trait shame increased in a notable minority of participants (29.8%). The activation of self-
conscious emotions with psychedelics deserves further attention as a challenging experience
subcategory that may be relevant to psychological outcomes. Such experiences could pose
a unique and context-dependent learning condition for both therapeutic and detrimental forms
of shame-related memory reconsolidation.
